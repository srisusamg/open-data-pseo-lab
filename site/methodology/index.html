Selection and ranking

For each country and indicator, the build requests the World Bank series and preserves the newest 15 non-null observations with their actual observation years. Rankings compare the latest of those source observations from highest to lowest. Missing values remain missing: the pipeline does not substitute zero, interpolate, forecast, or otherwise invent observations.

Derived metrics

Calculated metrics are displayed separately from World Bank source facts. Five-year and 10-year percentage change compare the latest observation with an observation from exactly five or 10 calendar years earlier. If that exact year is absent or the starting value is zero, the result is unavailable. CAGR uses the same exact endpoints and is calculated only when both endpoint values are positive.

Country comparisons

Comparison pages exist only for pairs in the configured allow-list. Latest values retain their own observation years; different years are flagged and never aligned through interpolation. Absolute difference is the first listed country minus the second in the indicator's own unit, while rate differences are labeled in percentage points. Percentage difference uses the second country as the denominator and is unavailable when that value is zero. The scale-neutral relative gap divides the absolute gap by the larger absolute value.

Deterministic summaries select from available scale leaders, exact-period percentage-change leaders, the largest relative gap, mathematically valid gap convergence or divergence, and data-freshness caveats. Gap change is calculated only when both countries have positive values at the same exact start and end years. Missing facts produce no claim.

What Changed pages

Change windows, endpoint tolerance, minimum historical span, and publication thresholds are configuration-driven. For each requested endpoint, the pipeline selects the nearest real observation within the configured one-year tolerance; a tie at the start prefers the earlier observation and a tie at the end prefers the later observation. Metrics without suitable endpoints or enough span are skipped. There is no interpolation, forecasting, or substitution with zero.

Source facts remain separate from absolute change, relative percentage change, percentage-point change, CAGR, direction, span, and phase-rate calculations. Percentage indicators use percentage-point change as their primary representation. Relative change is unavailable for a zero baseline, and CAGR is available only for positive endpoints.

A deterministic insight layer creates structured candidates, assigns explicit priorities, removes redundant claims, prefers diversity, and renders selected evidence through fixed sentence templates. Acceleration or deceleration requires enough observations and must exceed the configured rate threshold; standout claims require the leading non-percentage relative change to be at least 1.75 times the next. Pages publish only when at least three metrics, valid provenance, sufficient spans, and three insight candidates are available. Generated and intentionally skipped pages are recorded in the page quality report.

Limitations

Countries and indicators can have different latest observation years because reporting schedules vary. The year shown beside every value is part of the result and should be considered when making comparisons. Indicator definitions and revisions remain under World Bank stewardship.
