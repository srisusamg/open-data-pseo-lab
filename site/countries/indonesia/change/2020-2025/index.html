What changed?

How Indonesia changed, 2020–2025

Requested period: 2020–2025. Actual observation years are shown for every indicator; values are never interpolated.

Summary

Internet users increased by 19.1 percentage points. GDP recorded the largest relative increase among the tracked non-percentage indicators, changing by 36.5%. GDP per capita grew at an annualized rate of approximately 5.6% over the observed period. All 5 tracked indicators increased over their observed periods. Population increased from 274,814,866 to 285,721,236. The exact start and end observation years vary by indicator and are shown below.
