How Vietnam changed, 2020–2025

Requested period: 2020–2025. Actual observation years are shown for every indicator; values are never interpolated.

Summary

Internet users increased by 13.8 percentage points. GDP recorded the largest relative increase among the tracked non-percentage indicators, changing by 48.5%. GDP per capita grew at an annualized rate of approximately 7.5% over the observed period. 4 tracked indicators increased, while 1 tracked indicator declined. Population increased from 98,079,191 to 101,598,527. The exact start and end observation years vary by indicator and are shown below.
