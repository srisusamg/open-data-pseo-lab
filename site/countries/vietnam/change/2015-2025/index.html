What changed?

How Vietnam changed, 2015–2025

Requested period: 2015–2025. Actual observation years are shown for every indicator; values are never interpolated.

Summary

Internet users increased by 39.1 percentage points. GDP recorded the largest relative increase among the tracked non-percentage indicators, changing by 115.1%. GDP per capita grew at an annualized rate of approximately 7.0% over the observed period. Life expectancy at birth increased faster in the second half of the observed period. Population increased more slowly in the second half of the observed period. All 5 tracked indicators increased over their observed periods.
