What changed?

How China changed, 2015–2025

Requested period: 2015–2025. Actual observation years are shown for every indicator; values are never interpolated.

Summary

Internet users increased by 41.3 percentage points. GDP recorded the largest relative increase among the tracked non-percentage indicators, changing by 72.8%. GDP per capita grew at an annualized rate of approximately 5.4% over the observed period. Life expectancy at birth increased more slowly in the second half of the observed period. All 5 tracked indicators increased over their observed periods. Population increased from 1,379,860,000 to 1,406,585,000.
