What changed?

How China changed, 2020–2025

Requested period: 2020–2025. Actual observation years are shown for every indicator; values are never interpolated.

Summary

Internet users increased by 21.5 percentage points. GDP per capita recorded the largest relative increase among the tracked non-percentage indicators, changing by 30.4%. GDP grew at an annualized rate of approximately 5.4% over the observed period. 3 tracked indicators increased, while 2 tracked indicators declined. Population declined from 1,411,100,000 to 1,406,585,000. The exact start and end observation years vary by indicator and are shown below.
