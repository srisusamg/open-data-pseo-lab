How Brazil changed, 2020–2025

Requested period: 2020–2025. Actual observation years are shown for every indicator; values are never interpolated.

Summary

Internet users increased by 3.1 percentage points. GDP recorded the largest relative increase among the tracked non-percentage indicators, changing by 54.5%. GDP per capita grew at an annualized rate of approximately 8.7% over the observed period. All 5 tracked indicators increased over their observed periods. Population increased from 208,660,842 to 212,812,405. The exact start and end observation years vary by indicator and are shown below.
