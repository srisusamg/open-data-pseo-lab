How United States changed, 2015–2025

Requested period: 2015–2025. Actual observation years are shown for every indicator; values are never interpolated.

Summary

Internet users increased by 20.1 percentage points. GDP recorded the largest relative increase among the tracked non-percentage indicators, changing by 68.2%. GDP per capita grew at an annualized rate of approximately 4.7% over the observed period. All 5 tracked indicators increased over their observed periods. Population increased from 321,815,121 to 341,784,857. The exact start and end observation years vary by indicator and are shown below.
