What changed?

How United States changed, 2020–2025

Requested period: 2020–2025. Actual observation years are shown for every indicator; values are never interpolated.

Summary

Internet users increased by 4.3 percentage points. GDP recorded the largest relative increase among the tracked non-percentage indicators, changing by 43.9%. GDP per capita grew at an annualized rate of approximately 6.9% over the observed period. Population increased faster in the second half of the observed period. All 5 tracked indicators increased over their observed periods. The exact start and end observation years vary by indicator and are shown below.
